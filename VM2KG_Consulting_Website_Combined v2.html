What kinds of challenges can you help with?

We focus on project and programme delivery, digital transformation planning, development initiatives and PMO or governance improvements. An initial conversation helps establish whether our support matches your needs.

Can you support an existing project?

Yes. An engagement can focus on reviewing delivery arrangements, clarifying priorities, improving reporting or addressing coordination challenges. The scope depends on your project’s circumstances.

How is an engagement scoped and priced?

We begin by discussing your objectives, timeline and available information. The proposed scope, deliverables, responsibilities and fees are then agreed before work begins.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Remote collaboration can support many advisory and project management activities. Any on-site requirements, travel arrangements and associated costs are discussed when defining the engagement.

What should I prepare for an initial discussion?

A brief description of your challenge, desired outcomes, key dates and current constraints is enough to begin. Please avoid sending confidential documents until an appropriate sharing arrangement has been agreed.

Project Readiness Checklist

Discuss these questions with your team. Any uncertain answer is a useful starting point for further planning.

  1. Have we clearly defined the problem or opportunity?
  2. Are the intended outcomes and beneficiaries understood?
  3. Is there an accountable sponsor with decision-making authority?
  4. Have we agreed what is included and excluded from scope?
  5. Are the budget, people and skills realistically available?
  6. Have we identified key stakeholders and how to involve them?
  7. Are the timeline, milestones and dependencies achievable?
  8. Have we assessed major risks, assumptions and constraints?
  9. Are success measures and reporting responsibilities defined?
  10. Is there a plan for handover, ownership and ongoing benefits?
